IMMUNE EXCHANGE

A centerless, signed antibody bus for gates. A gate that refuses an attack signs a bundle of the raw bytes and publishes it; every subscriber verifies it came from a pinned peer, then re-runs the attack on its own gate before trusting a byte. A zero-day caught anywhere immunises everyone. booting kernel…

The relay is untrusted: it can’t forge a bundle, swap its antigens, or lift a signature — and even a perfect bundle only gets a subscriber to re-run an attack, never to adopt on anyone’s say-so. A packet a gate would admit is never adopted (no false immunity). Same kernel the tests and CI gate — nothing mocked.
